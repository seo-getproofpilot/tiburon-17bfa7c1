Our story

Two divers, one island, and a rum worth bringing home.

Tiburon was started by Basil and Elizabeth DeStefano. Basil spent 37 years as a finance director in the Chicago area, and the two of them were scuba divers who kept coming back to the Caribbean. Elizabeth found Belize through their diving, and they fell for its reef, its people and its rum.

So they made their own. Tiburon is molasses-based, blended from barrels of different ages and re-casked in bourbon oak for a second maturation. The warm Belize climate does the rest, giving a young rum the character of a much older one.

“The most fun I've ever had.”

Basil DeStefano, President, on building Tiburon

White Tip infusions

Half a bottle, some fruit, and a few days.

At 92 proof, White Tip pulls flavour out of fresh fruit and spice fast. These are the recipes we pour at the tasting rooms.

    Pineapple Cinnamon

      The house syrup

      Boil 3 cups of water, add 2 big spoons of sugar, a quarter cup of natural honey and a teaspoon of vanilla extract. Bring it back to a boil, simmer a few minutes, then let it rest off the heat, but not too long or it turns watery. Mix it into your infused rum until it is as strong as you like.

      Conservation

      $1 BZ from every bottle goes to protecting the Belize reef.

      The ocean is where Tiburon started, so we have teamed with Oceana, whose science and policy work protects marine life and habitats from overfishing and destructive fishing gear.

      29 ft

      Out on the water, Tiburon helped deliver a 29-foot Bradley Pelican marine vessel to the Belize Coast Guard to support conservation work on the coast.
